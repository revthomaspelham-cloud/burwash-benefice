**Family Support Work**, our diocesan charity, is appealing for gifts to make Christmas a little more special for families who are struggling. The list of families it helps has grown this year.

Everything given goes into boxes that are delivered to families in the weeks before Christmas. This year we're collecting extras for the Christmas meal.

Please leave whatever you can in the large box just inside the church door at **the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin & St Nicholas, Etchingham** or **St Bartholomew's, Burwash**, and we'll make sure it's handed over quickly.

- **Welcome:** tins, jars and packets, such as fruit, meat, fish, vegetables, sauces, cake, desserts, sweets and Christmas crackers.
- **Please don't bring:** fresh food or alcohol.

The last day for donations is **Saturday 5 December**. Thank you!

You can read more about the charity on our [Family Support page](family-support.html).
