Do you enjoy singing? On Sunday 7 February everyone is welcome to join our choir for a Candlemas 'Come and Sing' service at St Bartholomew's, Burwash.

  • 2–4pmRehearsal with the choir
  • 4.30pmCandlemas service
  • AfterwardsTea at the Rectory

There's no audition and you don't need to read music. Just come along and enjoy singing together. Candlemas marks the presentation of Christ in the Temple, when Simeon greeted him as "a light to lighten the Gentiles", and it's one of the loveliest services of the year.

If you'd like to know more, get in touch with the Rector, or find out about joining the choir all year round.
