During Advent our **Travelling Crib** moves from place to place around the benefice, spending a day or so with each host before moving on. Burwash and Etchingham schools have already booked it in, and there are still **dates free**.

It's a lovely way to prepare for Christmas at home, with family, neighbours or friends. Anyone is welcome to host it, whether you come to church often or not.

If you'd like to have the crib for a day, please speak to **Vicky Patterson** at church, or contact the Rector at [{{rector_email}}](mailto:{{rector_email}}) and we'll put you in touch.
