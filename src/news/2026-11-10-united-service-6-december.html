On Sunday 6 December there will be a United Benefice service at 10.30am at the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin & St Nicholas, Etchingham. It is the final celebration of the church's 650th anniversary year.

We are delighted to welcome the Very Revd Edward Dowler, Dean of Chichester, to join us.

There will be no other morning services in the benefice that day, so do come and join us at Etchingham. Evensong at St Bartholomew's is at 4.30pm as usual.

Change to the usual pattern on 13 December

Because Etchingham is hosting on 6 December, the benefice service on Sunday 13 December at 10.30am will be at St Philip's, Burwash Weald, not Etchingham as it would usually be.

Parking at Etchingham is limited at the front of the church, but the Bistro car park at the station is available.
