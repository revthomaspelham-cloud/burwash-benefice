On the second Sunday of the month, our benefice Communion usually alternates between St Philip's and Etchingham.

This month, Etchingham is hosting the [United Benefice service on Sunday 6 December](news-2026-11-10-united-service-6-december.html), so the benefice Communion on **Sunday 13 December at 10.30am** will be at **St Philip's, Burwash Weald**. Everyone is welcome.

You'll find every service on our [calendar](services.html#calendar).
