From Saturday 28 November to Thursday 3 December, St Bartholomew's Church in Burwash will be filled with Christmas trees for our Christmas Tree Festival.

Do come and see them, and bring family and friends. On Sunday 29 November at 4.30pm we'll hold our Toy Crib and Carol Service among the trees.

Parking near the church is limited, so please use the village car park next to the Bear Inn.
