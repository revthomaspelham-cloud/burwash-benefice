The **Burwash Festive Evening** is on **Wednesday 2 December, from 4pm to 8pm**, with the High Street Christmas lights shining for the occasion.

It's also the last day of our [Christmas Tree Festival](news-2026-11-10-christmas-tree-festival.html), and **St Bartholomew's will be open** during the evening. Do pop in to see the trees on your way along the High Street. Everyone is welcome.
