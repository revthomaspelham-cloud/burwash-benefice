Family support

If things are hard at home, you don't have to manage alone.

Family Support Work (FSW) is the Diocese of Chichester's own charity, and the main charity our churches support. Since 1890 it has helped children and families across Sussex through difficult times. These include money worries, illness, disability, bereavement, family breakdown and domestic abuse.

FSW's practitioners visit families at home. They listen, help with practical things like forms and appointments, and can provide food, clothing and household essentials when they're needed. They stay alongside a family for as long as it takes. Their help is free, confidential and for families of all faiths and none.
