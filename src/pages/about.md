## Who we are

The Benefice of Burwash, Burwash Weald and Etchingham brings together three parish churches in the High Weald of East Sussex:

- [St Bartholomew's, Burwash](burwash.html) · [Services this week](services.html#calendar)
- [St Philip's, Burwash Weald](burwash-weald.html) · [Services this week](services.html#calendar)
- [The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin & St Nicholas, Etchingham](etchingham.html) · [Services this week](services.html#calendar)

Each parish has its own church council, and we share one Rector and worship together often.

We are part of the Church of England, in the [Diocese of Chichester](https://www.chichester.anglican.org/). You can read about [our vision](index.html#vision) for the benefice on our home page.

## Our faith

We are parishes of the Church of England, part of the worldwide Anglican Communion. We worship one God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and we seek to follow Jesus Christ and to share his love with our villages.

The Church of England's teaching is grounded in the Holy Scriptures, and in the teaching of the early Church that agrees with them. It is summed up in the historic creeds, the [Apostles' Creed](https://www.churchofengland.org/faith/what-we-believe/apostles-creed) and the [Nicene Creed](https://www.churchofengland.org/faith/what-we-believe/nicene-creed), which we say together in our services, as Christians have done for centuries.

The Church's doctrine is set out in particular in the [Thirty-nine Articles of Religion](https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer/articles-religion), the [Book of Common Prayer](https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer) and the Ordinal. Every priest affirms this faith when they are ordained and again when they take up a new post. Our worship draws on both the Book of Common Prayer and Common Worship, with the sacraments of Baptism and Holy Communion at its heart.

## Our Rector

<img alt="Revd Tom Pelham, Rector" class="rector-photo" src="images/rector.jpg"/>

**Revd Tom Pelham** has been Rector of Burwash, Burwash Weald and Etchingham since 2021.

Music has always been a big part of Tom's life. He studied music at Bristol, specialising in composition, and was Director of Music in Bradford on Avon while working as a database developer. Sensing a call to ordination, he trained at Ripon College Cuddesdon from 2015. He was ordained deacon in 2018, served his curacy in Poole, and was ordained priest in 2019.

Tom is married to Sarah and they have two children. He is a governor of the church schools in Burwash and Etchingham. When he gets the chance he enjoys cycling, sailing, running and building models, and he plays cricket for Burwash.
