Baptisms and weddings: privacy notice

How the Rector looks after your information.

Baptism enquiry Wedding enquiry

Who we are

When you arrange a baptism or a wedding with us, the Rector, Revd Thomas Pelham, is the data controller for your information (not the PCC). Questions: {{rector_email}} or {{rector_phone}}.

What we hold and why

We hold the details you give us on the enquiry form, by email or in person, so that we can prepare for and take the service, and complete the church registers as the law requires. For weddings this includes the legal preliminaries (banns or licence).

Who sees it

The Rector, and, only as needed to arrange the service, the verger, the administrative assistant, the organist and the bellringers. For weddings, the details needed for banns or a licence may also be shared with other parishes or the diocesan registry. We never sell your information or use it for fundraising.

The registers

Baptisms and weddings are entered in the church registers. These are permanent legal records. Entries cannot be deleted, and the registers are eventually deposited at The Keep, the East Sussex record office, where older registers can be seen by the public.

How long we keep it

The Rector keeps the digital copy of your enquiry form, and related correspondence, for 10 years and then deletes it. This helps when checking family records, for example when a brother or sister is baptised later. The registers themselves are kept permanently.

Letters and emails

Personal letters and emails to the Rector are archived until the end of his time as Rector here, unless you ask for them to be deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, or ask us to correct it. We can delete the digital copy of your form on request, but not a register entry. Complaints: please talk to the Rector first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
