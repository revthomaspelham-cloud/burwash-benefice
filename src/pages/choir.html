The Choir

New voices of all ages and experience are always welcome.

The choir leads our worship with beauty and devotion, drawing on the riches of traditional hymns and choral music through the seasons of the church year.

We're a friendly, welcoming group and we'd love to grow. You don't need to read music or have sung in a choir before: we're happy to teach, and you'll pick things up quickly alongside others. If you sang at school years ago, or simply enjoy singing in the shower, come and give it a try.

Children and young people are very welcome too. Our choir leaders are safeguarding trained, and we follow the Church of England's safeguarding guidance at every rehearsal and service.

There's no audition and no pressure. Just come along to a rehearsal, have a go and see how you get on.
