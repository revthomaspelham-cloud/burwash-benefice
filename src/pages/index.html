Our churches

Each of our three churches has its own village, history and character. Do come and visit.

Burwash

St Bartholomew's

At the heart of the village, looking out over the Dudwell Valley.

Burwash Weald

St Philip's

A peaceful spot on Stonegate Road, opposite the Burwash Weald pavilion.

Etchingham

The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin & St Nicholas

A Grade I listed church beside the railway station.
