Bellringing

A centuries-old village tradition, and a great way to make friends.

Our bellringers keep alive a centuries-old village tradition, calling the community to worship and marking life's great moments with sound and rhythm.

We're a friendly band and we're keen to grow. New learners are just as welcome as experienced ringers. You don't need to be musical or especially strong, as ringing is about timing and technique, not muscle. We're happy to teach from scratch, and we'll take you through it step by step at your own pace.

Young people are welcome to learn too. Our tower captain and teachers are safeguarding trained, and we follow the Church of England's safeguarding guidance in the tower.

Come up the tower one Tuesday evening to watch, meet the band and have a go if you'd like.
