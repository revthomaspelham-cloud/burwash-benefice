Services & events

We follow a monthly cycle of services across all three churches. Families and children are welcome at any of them.

Our usual Sunday pattern

This is our normal monthly pattern. At festivals and special occasions things change, so it's always worth checking the calendar below.

1st Sunday

  • 9.30amBCP Holy Communion
    Etchingham
  • 10.30amParish Communion
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • 10.30amMorning Praise
    St Philip's, Burwash Weald
  • EveningBCP Evensong
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash

2nd Sunday

  • 9.30amMorning Prayer
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • 10.30amBenefice Communion, one service for all three parishes
    Etchingham or St Philip's
  • EveningPraise@thePavilion
    Burwash Common Pavilion

3rd Sunday

  • 9.30amBCP Holy Communion
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • 10.30amParish Communion
    Etchingham
  • 10.30amParish Communion
    St Philip's, Burwash Weald
  • EveningEvening Prayer
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash

4th Sunday

  • 9.30amMatins
    Etchingham
  • 10.30amBenefice Family Communion, one service for all three parishes
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • EveningEvening Prayer
    St Philip's, Burwash Weald

Evening services are at 4.30pm or 6pm depending on the time of year: check the calendar. When a month has a 5th Sunday, we usually hold a special service; details are in the calendar.
