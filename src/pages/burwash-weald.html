St Philip's

About us

St Philip's stands in a peaceful spot on Stonegate Road, opposite the Burwash Weald pavilion car park. We are a welcoming and eclectic worshipping community serving Burwash Common and Burwash Weald.

Services

Our worship ranges from contemplative evening prayer to sung parish communion.
