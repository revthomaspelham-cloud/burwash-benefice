Ask for prayer

Whatever is on your heart, we'd be glad to pray with you.

Every request is prayed for at our Thursday Communion, 10am at St Bartholomew's, Burwash. You're welcome to join us. You can give your name, or stay anonymous. Either way, your request will be prayed for.

Names in your request may be read out in church. So if you mention someone else, please make sure they're happy to be prayed for by name.

Thank you. Your request has been sent, and we'll pray for it at Thursday Communion.

Would you rather talk?

Call the Rector on {{rector_phone}} or email {{rector_email}}. If you'd like someone to visit, for yourself or someone you know, our pastoral team would be glad to.

Your information

Your request goes by email to the Rector, Revd Thomas Pelham, who is responsible for it. It isn't stored on this website or shared with anyone else, apart from being prayed for in church as described above. The Rector deletes prayer requests within a month. You can ask for yours to be deleted sooner, or ask any questions, at {{rector_email}}.
