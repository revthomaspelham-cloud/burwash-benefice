Get involved

There are lots of ways to be part of church life, and you'd be very welcome in any of them. No experience needed.

Choir

Wednesdays, 6pm, St Bartholomew's

Sing hymns and choral music through the church year. All ages and voices welcome, and we're happy to teach.

Find out more →

Bellringing

Tuesdays, 7.30pm, St Bartholomew's tower

Keep a centuries-old tradition alive. Beginners welcome, and training is provided.

Find out more →

Sidespeople

Sidespeople are the friendly faces at the door: welcoming people as they arrive, handing out books and service sheets, helping newcomers find their way, and taking the collection. It's a lovely way to help, and you can join the rota as often or as rarely as suits you.

Contact the Rector to join the rota.

Readers

Reading the Bible aloud in services helps everyone hear God's word. If you'd be happy to read now and then, we'd love to add you to the rota. We'll let you know the readings in advance, so you can prepare.

Contact the Rector to join the rota.

3Cs: Coffee, Cake & Chat

Mondays, 10am–12 noon, St Bartholomew's

Our weekly café in church. Drop in for a cup of coffee, a slice of cake and a chat with friends old and new. Everyone welcome, no need to book.

Pastoral visiting team

Our pastoral team visits people at home, in hospital or in care, offering friendship, a listening ear and prayer. If you'd like a visit, for yourself or someone you know, or you'd like to join the team, please get in touch.

Contact the Rector

House groups

Small groups meet in people's homes to read the Bible, pray and talk about faith together. They're a great way to grow in faith and get to know people, whether you're new to it or have been coming for years.

Contact the Rector to find a group.
