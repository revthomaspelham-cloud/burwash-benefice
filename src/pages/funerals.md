A funeral in church offers space for grief and gratitude: a time to commend someone you love to God's care, surrounded by prayer, beauty and peace.

Services can be quiet and simple or more formal. Each is shaped to reflect the person who has died and the hope we share as a church.

You can make arrangements directly with us or through your funeral director. We'll take care of everything to do with the church and churchyard.

After a cremation, we can also bury ashes in our churchyards. See [Burial of ashes](burial-of-ashes.html).
