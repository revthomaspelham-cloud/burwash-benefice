A church wedding weaves your vows into something larger: the beauty of worship, the presence of God, and the prayers of a community that will remember you. Whether simple or grand, it's a joyful beginning shaped by tradition, faith, and love that endures.

We'll guide you through the legal side, including banns or licence, your qualifying connection or residence, and the forms and fees. We'll also help you choose music and readings.

To marry in one of our churches, one of you needs a legal connection with it: for example, living in the parish, having been baptised or confirmed here, or a parent or grandparent having married here. The Church of England explains [who can marry in church](https://www.churchofengland.org/life-events/church-weddings/who-can-marry-church). If you're not sure, just ask.
