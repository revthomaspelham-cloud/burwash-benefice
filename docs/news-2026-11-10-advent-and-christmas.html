Advent and Christmas services

Advent and Christmas are a wonderful time to come to church, whether you're with us every week or haven't been for years. Everyone is welcome at all of these services.

  • Sun 22 Nov, 4.30pmChristingle
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • Sun 29 Nov, 4.30pmCrib, Toy and Carol Service: please bring a toy for Family Support Work
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • Sun 6 Dec, 10.30amUnited benefice service, 650th anniversary celebration with the Dean of Chichester
    Etchingham
  • Sun 13 Dec, 4.30pmCrib, Carols and Christingle
    Etchingham
  • Sun 20 Dec, 4.30pmCarols by Candlelight
    St Bartholomew's, Burwash
  • Sun 20 Dec, 6pmCarols by Candlelight
    St Philip's, Burwash Weald

Christmas Eve and Christmas Day service times will be added here soon.

The Christmas Tree Festival will be at St Bartholomew's from 28 November to 2 December. You'll find every service on our calendar.
