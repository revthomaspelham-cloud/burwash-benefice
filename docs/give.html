Support our churches

Thank you for thinking of giving. Your generosity keeps our buildings open, our worship alive and our work in the villages going.

The easiest way to give regularly is through the Parish Giving Scheme. You set up a direct debit, and you can change or stop it whenever you like. If you're a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid adds 25% to your gift at no cost to you. Regular giving helps each parish plan and budget, and keeps you in control of your contributions.

Each of our three parishes has its own account, so choose the church you'd like to support.
