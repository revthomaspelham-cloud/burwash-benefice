Burial of ashes

A resting place in a churchyard you love.

After a cremation, many families want the ashes laid to rest somewhere that means something to them. We can bury ashes in all three of our churchyards: St Bartholomew's, Burwash; St Philip's, Burwash Weald; and Etchingham.

Who can be buried here

Ashes can be buried in our churchyards on the same basis as a burial:

  • people who lived in the parish
  • people who died in the parish
  • people on the church electoral roll

We can also accept ashes where someone had a strong connection to Burwash, Burwash Weald or Etchingham, or where there's a family grave here.

Family graves

If a member of the family is already buried in one of our churchyards, ashes can usually be interred in that grave.

The ashes area

For people with a looser connection, the Rector may allow ashes to be buried in a general ashes area, where there is a memorial bench. Please talk to the Rector about this.

The service

Burials of ashes are usually short, simple services with family and friends at the graveside. You can arrange one directly with the Rector, or through your funeral director.

Containers

We're sorry, but we can only accept ashes in a biodegradable container. Your funeral director can help with this.
