What's on

Christmas Tree Festival at St Bartholomew's

Posted: 9 November 2026

St Bartholomew's Church in Burwash will be filled with Christmas trees for our Christmas Tree Festival. Do come and see them, and bring family and friends.

  • Sat 28 NovFestival opens, with evening entertainment from 6pm
  • Sun 29 Nov, 4.30pmCrib, Toy and Carol Service among the trees. Please bring a toy to give to Family Support Work.
  • Mon 30 Nov & Tue 1 DecClosed
  • Wed 2 DecLast day of the festival

Parking near the church is limited, so please use the village car park next to the Bear Inn.
