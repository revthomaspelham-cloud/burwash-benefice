Ask for prayer

Whatever is on your heart, we'd be glad to pray with you.

Every request is offered at our Thursday Communion, 10am at St Bartholomew's, Burwash. You're welcome to join us. You can give your name, or stay anonymous. Either way, your request will be offered.

Names in your request may be read out in church. So if you mention someone else, please make sure they're happy to be named.

Thank you. Your request has been sent, and it will be offered at Thursday Communion.

Would you rather talk?

Call the Rector on 07856 256 174 or email revthomaspelham@gmail.com. If you'd like someone to visit, for yourself or someone you know, our pastoral team would be glad to.

Your information

Your request goes by email to the Rector, Revd Thomas Pelham, who is responsible for it. It isn't stored on this website or shared with anyone else, apart from being offered in church as described above. The Rector deletes prayer requests within a month. You can ask for yours to be deleted sooner, or ask any questions, at revthomaspelham@gmail.com.
