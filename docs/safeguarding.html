Safeguarding

Keeping everyone safe in our church communities.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. Then contact your local children's or adult social care services.

We are committed to protecting children, young people and adults from harm. We follow the House of Bishops' safeguarding guidance, and each of our parishes has a Parish Safeguarding Officer.

If you are worried about possible harm to anyone, please contact the Parish Safeguarding Officer for your parish, or the Diocesan Safeguarding Advisor.
